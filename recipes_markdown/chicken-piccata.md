## Chicken Piccata.
One of my favorites. Highly recommend practicing "mise-en-place" for this recipe as things come together quick.

### The Gist
Chicken with caper and white wine sauce.

---

### Detailed Recipe
#### Ingredients
 - 2 Chicken breasts
 - 1/4c flour
 - 1/3 - 1/2 a white onion
 - Garlic, 2-5 cloves
 - 2-3tbsp capers
 - 1/2c White Wine
 - Optionally, chili flakes
 - Chicken stock, maybe 1-2c
 - 1-2tbsp lemon juice
 - Butter (a lot)
 - Pasta for serving (I like linguini)
 - Optionally, parsley

#### Steps
1. If you are making noodles, begin boiling water now. Cook and drain noodles, reserving a cup of pasta water.
2. Cut chicken breasts in half and pound flat.
3. Season with salt and pepper, then lightly coat in flour.
4. Heat oil in skillet and place chicken in skillet.
5. Get a nice sear on the chicken, don't need to cook all the way through. Remove from heat.
6. Add onion and garlic to skillet. Reserve some garlic for the noodles. Add more oil if needed and chili flakes if desired.
7. After things soften, add capers and a splash of brine.
8. Add white wine and cook down by 1/2 - 2/3.
9. Add chicken stock and bring to simmer. You want enough liquid in the pan to simmer the chicken again, but you want it to eventually be a sauce - so don't add too much, but don't add too little such that you don't have enough volume of sauce. Start with a cup.
10. Add chicken back to simmering liquid.
11. Once chicken is cooked, add lemon juice to taste.
12. Turn heat off and add a ton of butter - a few tbsp probably. Remove chicken from pan for plating.
13. For the noodles, heat butter, olive oil, and garlic in pan. Optionally, chili flakes.
14. Add cooked noodles and pasta water to pan, tossing to form a rich, creamy, garlicky sauce.
